description: Attachapick is a portable guitar pick storage system that integrates with a standard capo, allowing musicians to securely store and quickly access picks while playing. It features a CNC-milled HDPE body with precision slots for reliable retention and easy removal, paired with a removable polycarbonate press-fit lid for bulk loading and jam prevention. The design was optimized for automated manufacturing, accounting for machining and robotic tolerances and utilizing HAAS CNC mini mills, UR5 robotic handling, conveyor transport, and robotic assembly for scalable production.
