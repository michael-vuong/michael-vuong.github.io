# Objective

Design and fabricate some form of light (decorative koi fish lamp) for the Lutron Lighting Competition! This particular project focused on combining lightweight materials with structural integrity to create an illuminated form, while combining the creativity and engineering.

---

# Design Steps
## 1. Concept Development
- Brainstormed and sketched shape of the lamp and base designs
- Developed the concept of a koi fish-inspired lamp emphasizing flowing, organic motion.
- Focused on capturing the natural curvature and elegance of koi through simplified, stylized geometry.
- Considered light diffusion, light intensity, and how the internal light source would interact with the outer surface.
- *Initial idea was to make the lamp slowly spin using a slow rpm motor, but didn't have enough time to implement.

## 2. Foundation
- 3D printed custom bevel gears for base.
- Cut plywood with band saw built a peg base for gear.
- Assemble gear/box base and inserted PVC pipe for lamp tube.

## 3. Structural Framework
- Fabricated the lamp’s primary structure using aluminum craft wire, forming both the flowing wave elements and the koi fish geometry.
- Integrated all components onto a central PVC pipe, providing a stable backbone for alignment, support, and assembly.


## 4. Surface Formation (Paper Mache)
- Applied layered paper mache over the wire frame to create the outer shell.
- Adjusted thickness to ensure durability while maintaining light transmission.
- Refined surface contours through multiple iterations to achieve smooth transitions and organic form.

## 5. Lighting Integration
- Integrated warm COB LED strips throughout the structure, weaving them through the form to achieve uniform illumination.
- Routed and secured wiring to maintain a clean layout while preserving the lamp’s internal structure.
- Connected the lighting system to a three-way switch for adjustable control.
- Powered the system using a 12V wall adapter, ensuring consistent and reliable operation.



# Results
Successfully fabricated a functional koi fish lamp combining sculpture and lighting.
Achieved stable structural performance through the wire-paper composite system.
Produced soft, diffused lighting that enhances the organic form.
Demonstrated effective integration of artistic design with material and structural constraints.
Final piece serves as both a functional light source and a visually expressive object.
